## Notation (Hallie's, confirmed)
- **`⟨n⟩`** — energy cost.
- **`$n`** — **price in the SHOP** (gold). **`$NA` = not purchasable / not draftable** — i.e. a token: it
  is *forged* or *generated*, never bought. (Switch Blade, Top Chop, Minion Machete, Mirror Shard…)
- **`U`** — upgrade. `6+U` = 6, plus more when upgraded. **`Upgrade ∞`** = infinitely upgradeable.
- **Vigor** — base-game power; adds to attack damage.
- **The puns are load-bearing.** *Queer Coat-ed*, *Scared Straight → Sober Straight*, *Gay Parry / Gay
  Paris → Gay Pass*. **Bad puns are part of it.** Do not sand them off.
