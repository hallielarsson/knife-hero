The Fool operates as a Tarot/ Fool's Journey

The Fools cards are Suited, and many of those powers activate and play off suits. The BASE strikes and defends of the fools
deck are suited with Wands, Cups, Coins and Swords

Strikes : Wands and Swords
Defends: Coins and Cups

Base Power:
If you have all four elements in play, CONSUME All Elements them and add a Quintessence.

Wands - When this is consumed, Inflict Weak
Coins - When this is consumed, gain 6 Block
Cups - When this is consumed, draw 1 Card
Swords- When this is consumed, deal 6 Damage
Quintessence - Gain 1 Str, 1 Def, Draw 1 Extra Card Per Turn
When This is Consumed, Deal 10 damage to a random Enemy and gain 10 block
